# Training plans from the simulations

_Generated by `python -m dbl patterns`. Every dangerous moment the defence faced was re-simulated by the posture-aware coach; corrections were tagged with an error type and ranked by the danger they would have removed. Lift = how much more often the error appears in dangerous moments than in ordinary defending. Real outcome = how often the attack actually shot within 10 s when the coach flags this error, against dangerous moments without it (same pitch third, both matches pooled). Priorities: errors that are both specific to danger (lift CI above 1) and linked to real shots come first._

### 1. Back-four line control — step up to squeeze — fixes *back line too deep: gap to midfield*

- **Objective:** When the ball goes backwards or sideways under pressure, the line steps up to keep the block compact.
- **Setup:** Same 8v6 as the drop drill; the attacking team must recycle the ball back every 3 passes.
- **Rules:** Defence scores when the distance between back line and midfield stays below 12 m at the moment of a forward pass.
- **Coaching points:** SEE: trigger = backward pass / pressured receiver; REACT: step up together, never alone; SHAPE: compact distance to midfield ≤ 12 m
- **Progression:** Live 10v10 with offside; video review of line height on the 3D viewer.
- **Track next match:** space between lines (line_gap), back-line height by phase
- **Watch in the 3D viewer:** 13:52 (B. Vriends, dangerous run), 03:37 (P. Kikianis, dangerous run), 47:15 (F. Talladira, pass in a shot possession)

### 2. Scan before the pass — ball and man — fixes *ball-watching: man not in view*

- **Objective:** Keep ball and man in one view; check the shoulder before every opponent pass.
- **Setup:** 4v4 + 3 neutrals rondo; behind each defender a coach holds a coloured bib for 1 s at random.
- **Rules:** A defender who calls the colour before the next pass earns a point; receivers free in front of an unscanned marker give the attack a point.
- **Coaching points:** SEE: shoulder check in the 3 s before each pass; SHAPE: side-on so the turn of the head is short; REACT: adjust distance after each check
- **Progression:** Transfer into 6v6 with blind-side runs; review with the 3D 'Player's eyes' camera.
- **Track next match:** dual vision at passes, shoulder checks per pass, blind-side runs conceded
- **Watch in the 3D viewer:** 35:23 (B. Vriends, dangerous run, pass in a shot possession), 68:50 (P. Kikianis, duel lost), 38:31 (E. Alagich, dangerous run)

### 3. Recovery race — get back goal-side — fixes *lost goal-side position*

- **Objective:** When the ball is played past you, recover on the inside line between your man and the goal before engaging.
- **Setup:** Half-pitch, 2 attackers vs 2 defenders + GK. Coach serves through-balls from the halfway line; defenders start 2 m ahead (on the wrong side) of their attacker.
- **Rules:** Defenders score a point by being goal-side of their man when he receives; attackers score normally. 6 reps × 4 sets.
- **Coaching points:** REACT: first step backwards on the pass, not after it; SHAPE: open hips to the goal, curve run to the inside line; SEE: check the runner over the shoulder while recovering
- **Progression:** Delay the defender's start by 0.5 s; add a third attacker arriving late.
- **Track next match:** goal_side rate of the receiver's marker at line-breaking passes (passes table)
- **Watch in the 3D viewer:** 35:24 (E. Alagich, pass in a shot possession), 42:12 (P. Kikianis, dangerous run, pass in a shot possession), 62:30 (B. Vriends, line-breaking pass)

### 4. Touch-tight in the box — fixes *too loose on his man*

- **Objective:** Reduce distance to your man as the ball approaches the danger zone (inside 25 m).
- **Setup:** Box + D: 4 attackers vs 4 defenders, 2 wide servers crossing/cutting back, GK.
- **Rules:** Marker must be within an arm's length (≈1.5 m) at the moment of the cross or the attack gets a bonus point.
- **Coaching points:** REACT: tighten when the server's head goes down; SEE: ball and man in one view (side-on); SHAPE: goal-side, between man and goal centre
- **Progression:** Add blind-side runs and late arrivals at the penalty spot.
- **Track next match:** marking_distance at passes into the box; receiver_free rate
- **Watch in the 3D viewer:** 35:23 (B. Vriends, dangerous run, pass in a shot possession), 47:21 (E. Alagich, pass in a shot possession), 47:15 (F. Talladira, pass in a shot possession)

### 4. First defender: close down and stop forward play — fixes *no pressure on the ball carrier*
