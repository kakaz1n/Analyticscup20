# /brag-slim plan v2: Defensive Body Language (simulation → training)

**What it is:** a defensive-analysis framework in a 3D match viewer (real SkillCorner skeletons, Gemini inside). It re-simulates how the whole defence should have moved, finds recurring errors, and turns them into drills on the real moments.
**For:** football analysts and coaches (PySport × SkillCorner Analytics Cup 2.0).
**Angle:** this version focuses on the new part: one real goal → the re-simulated defence → patterns across all dangerous moments → a drill → proof it is grounded.
**Hook:** Perth's winner, with "What should Brisbane have done?"
**Highlights:** (1) better-defence replay, with green simulated defenders and danger at the shot −48%; (2) 582 dangerous moments re-coached, errors ranked by their link to real shots (too loose: 26% vs 10%); (3) the drill played on the real moment in tactical mode, what happened vs the drill.
**Punchline:** validation (98% vs 73% pass completion, 18 m vs 17 m run) → "Simulate. Find the pattern. Train it."
**Tone:** polished. Viewer palette: navy #0b1220, amber #f59e0b, sim green #4ade80, Inter Display.
**Truthfulness:** every product frame is the real viewer, captured frame-exactly. All numbers are the framework's outputs (RESULTS.md §4–5, TRAINING.md). Player names appear only as in the public match data.
